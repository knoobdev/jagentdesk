# JAgentDesk v0.12.0

Bots rebuilt to work and look like Orca's: a Bots section in the sidebar, a bot page with its Home Chat and a Home · Connectors ·
Chats · Files panel, workers in their own worktrees, items with quick answers, Look around, Tidy memory, templates and mascots —
on desktop and on phones. Bots can now be reached from GitHub, GitLab and Telegram, and shared by link. This release also adds a
detail view with live output for background tasks, keeps the chat visible when the iPhone keyboard opens, tidies the trackers
above the composer into one row of pills, steadies the sidebar Usage summary, and brings in Paseo 0.17.6.

## Highlights

### Bots, the way Orca does them

- **Bots in the sidebar**, above Projects: each bot with its mascot, a status line (Needs you / Working / Paused / Idle) and its
  chats and workers nested underneath. The `+` menu has New bot, Templates and From a link.
- **The bot page** is its Home Chat, with a panel on the right:
  - **Home**: Needs you first (approvals, then questions, then connector fixes), items grouped by status with filters and
    "Cancel all" for stale ones, Automations ("New automation", Look around, Tidy memory) and Suggestions ("Look now").
  - **Connectors**, **Chats** (chat → worker tree) and **Files** (the bot's home folder).
  - **Settings**: agent and model, permissions ("Same as chats" by default), folder, schedule, items, notifications, sharing.
- **How a bot works**: it plans, starts workers in new worktrees for code changes, ends its turn and is woken when a worker
  finishes or asks. Work is tracked as items (planned, working, waiting, needs you, done, canceled); an item that needs you asks
  with quick answers and a recommended choice.
- **Look around** and **Tidy memory** are on by default, as in Orca. Every run shows in the bot's chat and Automations; you can
  turn either off in the bot's settings.
- **Soul and Memory**: edit SOUL.md (Read/Edit with conflict handling); Memory has "What it knows" and "History" with Undo.
- **Templates**: 11 ready-made bots (pull request review, dependency updates, release readiness, release notes and more).
- **Hand to bot**: hand a workspace to a bot with a note on what to do.
- **Review before done** (optional, off by default): BA, Tester and Pentester reviews must pass before an item is done.
- **Phones**: Bots in the drawer, the bot page with its panel sheet (Items / Chats / Files), Needs-you quick replies, long-press
  item actions and the New bot sheet.
- After a daemon restart a bot only continues a turn the restart cut off, with a notice in its chat; it is not woken otherwise.
- Bot mascots come from Orca under its MIT licence; the notice ships in every build.

### Reach bots from GitHub, GitLab and Telegram

- **GitHub**: a repository or organisation webhook (created with your `gh`), verified with its HMAC signature. Mention a bot with
  `/<bot>` in a comment; it answers in the thread. Events can start automations.
- **GitLab**: a project webhook verified with its secret token; merge requests, issues, pipelines and comments.
- **Telegram**: connect a BotFather token (kept in the daemon's secret store); direct messages and group mentions, replies in the
  thread, and a "Who can ask" list of Telegram users.
- Events travel through your own relay Worker; tokens and secrets never appear in logs. The 5-minute `gh` polling of earlier bots
  is gone.

### Share a bot by link

- Share a bot's soul, skills, automations and (optionally) memory as a link. The bundle is encrypted on your machine and the key
  lives only in the link; the receiver reviews what's inside before creating the copy. Update link and Stop sharing are in the
  bot's settings. Skills that contain a token can't be shared.
- **Needs a one-time setup on the relay Worker** (an R2 bucket); until it exists, Share says sharing is unavailable. See Upgrade
  notes.

### Background task details

- Tap a background task (the pill above the composer, the line under an agent in Recents) to see the full command, its status,
  how long it has run, its folder and **live output** in a read-only terminal, with Copy, Copy output, Jump to latest, Stop and
  Open in tab.
- Output is available for Claude and OpenCode; Codex doesn't expose it, so its details show everything except the output.

### Composer and phone

- The trackers above the composer (background tasks, subagents, tasks, plugin pills) are compact pills in one row aligned with
  the composer, instead of stacked full-width bars.
- **iPhone/Android: the chat no longer goes blank when the keyboard opens** (the transcript was lifted twice).
- Older rows in long chats render as soon as the visible range grows (Paseo #9887).

### From Paseo 0.17.6

- Settings are organised by **This device** and **Host**.
- Model picker: arrow keys move between models, providers and profiles; no more lost clicks or Enter; mouse clicks on Android.
- No flashes when restoring archived workspaces and agents, with an "Unarchiving" state.
- Background `git fetch` no longer spawns D-Bus daemons or hammers failing credentials; worktrees stay inside the configured
  worktrees root; Linux AppImage environment no longer leaks into shells.
- Timeline rows survive a rewind; a tab can't save a folder another tab removed.

## Fixes

- **Sidebar Usage summary** stays through reconnects, daemon restarts and slow reloads; it hides only when usage is turned off.
- Bot permission cards and tab-close fixes from v0.11.1 carry over to the new Bots page.

## Breaking changes / action needed

- **Bots look and work differently.** Existing bots keep their soul, memory, items and schedules; the three-role review is now
  an option (off). The old bot tabs screen is replaced by the new bot page.
- The relay Worker is redeployed with the hook routes for GitHub, GitLab and Telegram.
- Apps and daemons of v0.11.x and v0.12.0 work together; the new Bots features need both sides on v0.12.0.

## Upgrade notes

- To turn on bot sharing, create the bucket once on the Cloudflare account that runs your relay Worker:
  `npx wrangler r2 bucket create desk-relay-shares`, then redeploy the Worker from `packages/relay`.
- GitHub and GitLab webhooks and Telegram are set up per bot in its Connectors tab.

## Known limits

- Not checked against real GitHub, GitLab, Slack or Telegram accounts in testing (signed local simulators were used); the first
  real connection is the final check.
- Codex background tasks show no output (Codex has no API to read it).
- Bot automations still live on the bot rather than the shared Schedules list.
- Phone screens were checked on the web build at phone size and by compiling the iOS and Android bundles, not on a device.
- Not ported from Paseo 0.17.6: the operating system Tailscale toggle (JAgentDesk embeds its own Tailscale) and desktop
  auto-update pacing (JAgentDesk has no auto-updater yet).
